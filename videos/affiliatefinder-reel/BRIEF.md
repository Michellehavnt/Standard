## Intent

Turn the 2 October 2026 AffiliateFinder.ai product video into a 30-second vertical reel built for Instagram Reels and TikTok. The reel opens with a scroll-stopping hook card, cuts the strongest 20 to 24 seconds of the source footage into short segments, runs large word-synced captions over the footage, and closes with a brand card and a free-trial call to action. Loud, fast, confident. Built for sound-off viewing: every spoken line is readable on screen.

## Assets

- assets/source.mp4 — the source product video from Google Drive ("AffiliateFinder.ai - Affiliate Recruitment Software - 2 October 2026.mp4", 84 MB). NOT YET STAGED: the file could not be downloaded from this environment. See Notes.
- assets/logo.png — AffiliateFinder.ai wordmark, white on transparent (from AFPitch/logo.png). Use on dark backgrounds only.
- assets/transcript.srt — word or sentence level transcript of the source video. NOT YET STAGED: needed for captions and for choosing the cut.

## Customizations

- Hook card in the first 1.5 s with a bold claim and the brand colour.
- Footage reframed from 16:9 to 9:16 with punch-ins on the UI, never letterboxed.
- Word-synced captions, two to four words per cue, high contrast, lower-third safe zone.
- Closing card with the wordmark, one proof stat and "Start free" call to action.
